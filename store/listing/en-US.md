## Product name

## Description
```text
Scanload is the quickest way to get photos, videos, documents, and folders from your PC onto your phone. Drop files into the small window, scan the QR code with your phone’s camera, and the download starts. It works with iPhone and Android, and there’s nothing to install on your phone.

Two ways to send:
• Same Wi-Fi: Files go straight from your PC to your phone over your local network. Nothing is uploaded, and there’s no size limit.
• Internet: When your phone is on another network or using cellular data, Scanload uploads the file to a temporary sharing service you choose and shows the link as a QR code.

Link to QR turns any link or text, such as a website, an address, or a note, into a QR code you can scan or save as a PNG.

Scanload waits quietly in the system tray and can open when Windows starts. It’s available in English, Turkish, and Spanish, with light and dark appearance.
```
